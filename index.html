PushRogue

A cozy chain-reaction puzzle for Android. Rearrange the dominoes, make one push and watch the plan succeed, or fall apart in style.

Pip, a small red ant, and Iris, an explorer who never says no to a map, travel ten painted worlds. The Expedition is a roguelike run where time is your currency: pick a door, solve the level, spend the seconds you saved, and see how deep you get.

Free to try on Google Play. Made by a solo developer in Portugal.
